Another Level Expedition 2025: Mount Toubkal

Another Level have teamed up with Earthbound Expeditions to take you on a trekking expedition to the summit of Mt Toubkal, the highest peak of the Atlas Mountains in Morocco at 4167 metres.

Day 1 we will fly from a London airport direct to Marrakesh. We will be met by Earthbound Expeditions leader Badri and his team. First stop will be the village of Imlil (1,800 metres) and the guesthouse where we will be staying for the night. After an arrival meal we will get stuck straight into an acclimatisation trek around the village before returning for dinner and our first overnight stay.

Day 2 we will trek further up the mountain range reaching 3,200 metres. Our overnight accommodation will be at a local refuge with bunkbeds. Breakfast, lunch, and dinner provided.

Day 3 will be an early start to arrive at the summit a further 1,000 metres! We will return to the refuge to overnight again before heading back to Marrakesh the next day. Breakfast, lunch, and dinner provided.

Day 4 we begin our trek back to the guesthouse in Imlil before being driven back to the city where we will spend our last evening in a 3-star hotel (twin rooms). Breakfast and lunch provided.

Day 5 breakfast is provided at the hotel as are transfers back to the airport. Depending on our flight time back to the UK, we may have some time to explore the city of Marrakesh.
